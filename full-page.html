how we work together

These are part of every quote, not fine print. They're what keeps the price fixed and the timeline real.

Deposit starts the clock

The deposit is non-refundable and work begins when the transfer lands — not before. Your start date is reserved once it's paid.

Source files on final payment

Before the balance is settled you review previews. Editable source files are released with the final delivery.

A revision is one round

One consolidated set of feedback — a single document or a call — not three messages over three days. It's faster for you too.

Brief before kick-off

The brief form has to be completed before we start. No brief, no start date — it's the only way the first draft lands close.

15 days of silence = pause

If feedback goes quiet for more than 15 days the project pauses and restarts based on calendar availability.

One direction at a time

The number of proposals is fixed. I work on one direction at a time, because that's how you get to something good instead of something average.
